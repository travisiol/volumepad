# Launch posts (each < 280 chars; true of the code; no promised return)

1 — what it is
VOLUMEPAD is a pump.fun launchpad with a weekly contest. Launch a coin, and 30% of its creator fees go into a pot. When the week closes, the launchers of the top 3 coins by counted volume take it. $VOLUMEPAD [link]

2 — what launchers gain
Launch a coin on VOLUMEPAD and 60% of its creator fees are yours, withdrawable any time. Rank top 3 for the week and you also take 50/30/20 of the pot. Meme coins are risky and can go to zero. [link]

3 — how it works
Counted volume, not wash: each wallet counts max 25 SOL per coin per week, your own trades never count, 25+ traders to rank. Standings freeze at week end, prizes are sent on chain and listed with their tx. 10% of fees buys and burns $VOLUMEPAD. [link]
